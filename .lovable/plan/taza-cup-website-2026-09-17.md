# Taza Cup Website

## What I’ll build
- A complete five-page brand site: Home, Products, About Us, Contact Us, and Orders.
- A shared header and footer with clear navigation, mobile menu, and prominent ordering access.
- A guided order journey where customers choose quantities, enter pickup/contact details, review the order, and reach a final receipt confirmation.

## Visual direction
- Use the supplied Taza Cup artwork as the primary brand image and source for the favicon.
- Build a warm premium palette around ink black, burnished gold, ripe mango, berry red, leaf green, and soft cream.
- Pair an elegant editorial serif with a clean humanist sans-serif; use intentional whitespace, fine rules, asymmetric compositions, and restrained motion.
- Avoid generic card grids, gradient decoration, startup-style feature blocks, and excessive rounded containers.
- Create two cohesive studio product images for Mango Berry Cup and Tropical Gold Cup, with transparent fruit cups photographed against a dark premium setting.

## Page structure
- **Home:** full-width image-led opening, the exact tagline “Fresh. Fruity. Made for You.”, two requested actions, a clearly labeled reserved animation container, and a compact product preview.
- **Products:** two large editorial product presentations with ingredients, prices, and add-to-order controls.
- **About Us:** concise brand story, freshness principles, and an ingredient-focused visual composition.
- **Contact Us:** approachable contact form and clearly identified placeholder business details where real details were not provided.
- **Orders:** product quantities, customer and pickup details, review step, and a final receipt with order number, itemized totals, and confirmation message.

## Technical details
- Keep shared layout, product data, buttons, and order controls modular.
- Keep the draft order in the browser for this first version; no account, payment, or remote storage will be added.
- Add unique search and social metadata to every page.
- Verify the finished site at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, ordering steps, receipt state, and layout overlap checks.
