# Taza Cup Delight

Build a website for "Taza Cup," a fresh fruit cup business. 

Pages: Home, Products (only 2 products for now — mango-berry mix 

and a tropical mix, placeholder names/images fine for now), 

About Us, Contact Us, and an Orders page ending in a final 

receipt/confirmation screen.

Home page:

- Full-width hero section

- Below the hero, tagline: "Fresh. Fruity. Made for You."

- Two buttons under the tagline: "Checkout Our Products" (links 

  to Products page) and "Chat with Us" (placeholder for now — 

  will wire up a chatbot later)

- Leave a clearly marked placeholder section/div for a custom 

  animation I'll add separately (cup drops in, fruit falls into 

  it, lid closes, logo stamps on) — don't build this animation 

  yourself, just leave the container.

Brand: black background, gold/cream accents, warm and premium 

but still approachable — attached is our logo, match its color 

palette and feel.

Design direction: avoid generic AI-generated startup template 

look — no default rounded-corner card grids, no stock gradient 

blobs, no generic hero-with-3-buttons layout that looks like 

every other AI-built landing page. Make it feel like a real, 

designed food brand site — think specialty juice bar or premium 

dessert shop, not a SaaS template. Custom typography pairing, 

intentional whitespace, asymmetry where it helps, real product 

photography style placeholders rather than icon-driven sections.

Keep components clean and modular since I'll be extending this 

codebase afterward.
